# Mutiny

> Mutiny is the GTM assistant built for customer-facing work and workflow automation — vertical AI built specifically for sales execution. Founded 2018 (Y Combinator S18) by Jaleh Rezaei (CEO) and Nikhil Mathew (CTO), NYC. It generates on-brand, deal-ready assets (deal rooms, pitch decks, pricing proposals, business cases, ABM campaigns) and automates the repetitive work around deals, with interoperability with Claude and any MCP-compatible tool.

## Core Service Offerings

- Self-updating deal rooms: digital sales rooms with personalized content per account
- Pitch decks tailored to each account
- Competitive comparison pages tailored to the prospect's current vendor
- Business cases tailored to a specific buyer or buying committee
- Pricing proposals tailored to the deal
- AI-generated ABM campaigns: landing pages, microsites, ads, and multi-touch sequences for named accounts
- Meeting recaps and follow-up content
- Agents, skills, and routines that automate repetitive GTM workflows
- Brand, messaging, and playbook management for marketing teams
- Built-in analytics and reporting; automated follow-ups; deal coaching; pipeline review

## Technology Stack

- AI agent engine purpose-built for go-to-market
- Skills, routines, and playbook framework

- Customer-facing asset generation across documents, decks, pages, and deal rooms
